# Castle Siege — M4.2-B Defend design review (proposal, not built)

Status: **design review for Project Manager approval.** Nothing in this document is implemented:
`battle.py`, `characters.py`, `ai.py` and the rules are exactly as in M4.1/M4.2-A. Every number
below comes from the real damage formula (`characters.calculate_damage`) or from seeded runs of the
real game code; the throwaway scripts are not part of the project.

## 1. How Defend works now (verified in the code)

| Question | Answer (where) |
|---|---|
| What it does | The acting character takes half damage, after DEF, minimum 1 (`calculate_damage(..., defending)`; course rule C5). |
| Does it use the turn? | Yes, the whole team's turn: `Battle.perform_action("defend")` calls `_finish_turn` at once. A team has **one action per turn**, however many characters are alive. |
| How long | Until that team's next turn starts (`Battle._start_turn` clears it): exactly one enemy action. |
| Who can | Any living character, any number of times, no cooldown. |
| Whom it protects | Only that character. While any character of a team defends, enemy shots also do half damage to that team's blocks (Cover Guard, M3.2). Knight's Rally (a special, cooldown 4, +5 EXP) makes every living ally defend. |
| With cover | A standing block between the explosion and a character absorbs the whole blast for it, so Defend only matters when the shot actually reaches the defender (a direct hit, or no block in the way). |
| With Power Shot | Power Shot ignores DEF but is still halved against a defender (60 → 30 against a Knight). |
| EXP | Basic Defend earns 0 (a damaging hit earns 10); Rally and Repair earn 5. |
| AI | `AIController.wants_to_defend`: 10 / 5 / 0 % when unhurt, 40 / 50 / 60 % when a character is below half HP (Easy / Normal / Hard; course rule C4); never two defensive turns in a row. The weakest character defends, or a ready Knight Rallies. |
| Victory, round limit | Only eliminations win; after 30 complete rounds the match is a draw. Defend can never win; it can only delay. |
| Course constraints | C5 "Defend reduces damage by 50 %" and C4 "AI defends below 50 % HP" must stay true. |

## 2. Why Defend feels weak (the root cause)

Damage per hit (real formula; HP in brackets):

| Attacker → target | Normal | Target defending | ATK × 1.15 | ATK × 1.25 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Knight → Knight (140) | 18 | 9 | 22 | 25 |
| Knight → Archer (90) | 26 | 13 | 30 | 33 |
| Archer → Knight | 28 | 14 | 34 | 38 |
| Archer → Archer | 36 | 18 | 42 | 46 |
| Engineer → Knight | 22 | 11 | 27 | 30 |
| Engineer → Archer | 30 | 15 | 35 | 38 |

1. **Tempo.** A Defend gives up the team's only attack of the turn: an expected p × 18–36 HP
   (p = the chance the shot damages a character, about 0.5–0.7 behind cover). Its benefit is
   half of *one* enemy hit, on *one* character, and only if the enemy's next shot reaches that
   character: the enemy chooses its target and often hits cover or someone else.
2. **Losing a character costs no actions.** Each team acts once per turn whatever its size, so
   saving one character's HP is worth less than in games where every character acts.
3. **No EXP**, so a defender also falls behind in levels.
4. **Its main use is stalling.** The one situation where Defend is clearly right is running out
   the clock when losing (a draw beats a loss).

**Measured.**
- *Exact 1v1 duels* (both sides play perfectly; real damage and stats; hit chance p = 0.45, 0.6,
  0.8; no EXP/levels, which slightly favours Defend; 18 cases): having Defend available adds on
  average **+0.0002** to the win chance (at most +0.003). With both sides allowed to defend,
  best play uses it in 4 % of positions, mostly by the losing side to reach the round limit:
  draws rise to 0.068 in Knight v Knight (p 0.45), and the Archer losing to an Engineer defends
  30 % of its turns.
- *Seeded AI matches* (3v3, Normal v Normal, each AI with its own cover, 20 seeds × both first
  movers = 40 matches per row; LEFT's Defend frequency varied, RIGHT plays the current Normal):

| LEFT defends | LEFT wins | RIGHT wins | Draws | Rounds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| never | 25 (62 %) | 15 (38 %) | 0 | 19.1 |
| as now (5 % / 50 %) | 13 (32 %) | 25 (62 %) | 2 | 22.1 |
| heavily (30 % / 90 %) | 7 (18 %) | 25 (62 %) | 8 (20 %) | 25.5 |

So the player is right: under the current rules, defending more loses more and draws more.

## 3. The three options

| | A. Defend + next-attack bonus | B. Defend + counterattack | C. Defend + a resource |
|---|---|---|---|
| Idea | The defender's next normal Attack is stronger ("Guarded Strike") | If the defender is hit, it fires back at once | Defend stores a charge spent later on an attack |
| Tactical value | Situational, measurable (below) | High but random, and it turns Defend into an attack | Like A, but can be saved up |
| Complexity | Small: `calculate_damage` already takes a multiplier (Power Shot uses it) | Large: an automatic aimed shot inside the enemy's turn, a second projectile, a new animation path, AI that predicts it | Medium: a new counter on every character, UI, save/spend rules, AI rules |
| Animation | None new (the run to the cannon is reused) | New (a return shot mid-turn) | None new |
| AI | Already values it: the AI imagines shots with the real rules, so a stronger shot is preferred automatically | Needs new prediction logic | Needs a spending policy |
| Balance risk | Overuse / stalling if the bonus is large (measured; fixed by one rule, below) | Defend could become the best "attack" | Hoarding, then burst turns |
| Course fit | Keeps C4 and C5 | Keeps C5, but blurs "one action per turn" | Keeps C4 and C5 |

**B** and **C** cost far more than they return for a one-semester Pygame project. **A** is the
simplest, but the size of the bonus matters, as the next section shows.

## 4. Recommendation: Option A, "Guarded Strike", with one anti-stall rule

1. **Defend is unchanged:** the defender takes half damage until its team's next turn; Cover
   Guard unchanged (C5 still holds).
2. **Guarded Strike:** a basic Defend also gives that character Guarded Strike for its team's
   **next turn only**. If that character makes a normal **Attack** in that turn, the shot uses
   **ATK × 1.25** (then DEF, minimum 1, halved against a defender: the same formula Power Shot
   uses with × 1.5). Block damage is unchanged. The bonus is used up by that Attack.
3. **Expiry:** it ends with that turn whatever happens: another character acts, the defender
   uses its special, or Defend is chosen again. It is never stored or stacked.
4. **Not with specials:** Power Shot keeps its own × 1.5 and does not use or add the bonus; Rally
   gives no Guarded Strike (it has its own value and cooldown).
5. **Anti-stall:** **a team can't Defend two turns in a row.** The computer has always played
   this way; for players it removes the stalling loop. DEFEND is greyed out with the reason.
6. **EXP unchanged:** Defend 0; the Guarded Strike hit earns the normal 10 (+20 per defeat).

**Why × 1.25 and the anti-stall rule** (exact 1v1 duels as in section 2, 18 cases each):

| Variant | Mean gain of having Defend | Max gain | Mean draw rate, both may Defend (none with Attack only) | Defend chosen | Cases with a side defending > 30 % |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Now | +0.0002 | +0.003 | 0.004 (max 0.068) | 4.0 % | 2 |
| Now + anti-stall | +0.0000 | +0.000 | 0 | 2.4 % | 0 |
| ATK × 1.15 | +0.0026 | +0.013 | 0.018 | 12.4 % | 5 |
| ATK × 1.15 + anti-stall | +0.0024 | +0.013 | 0 | 5.0 % | 0 |
| ATK × 1.20 | +0.018 | +0.054 | 0.064 | 32.1 % | 8 |
| ATK × 1.20 + anti-stall | +0.018 | +0.054 | 0 | 6.8 % | 0 |
| ATK × 1.25 | +0.025 | +0.060 | 0.070 | 28.1 % | 9 |
| **ATK × 1.25 + anti-stall** | **+0.025** | **+0.060** | **0** | **6.1 %** | **0** |

- **× 1.15 (the brief's starting point) is too small to matter:** +0.0026 win chance on average,
  and in the AI matches it changed nothing (LEFT 62 / 30 / 18 % for never / as now / heavily,
  the same as without it). In damage terms it adds 4–6 HP to one later hit (× p), against a
  whole lost attack.
- **× 1.25 gives Defend a real, situational value** (up to +6 points of win chance where it fits,
  mostly against high-DEF Knights and Engineers; none in Archer mirrors, which end in 3 hits).
- **Without the anti-stall rule, a large bonus creates loops:** at × 1.20–1.25 best play defends
  28–32 % of the time and draws rise to 6–7 %. With the rule, Defend is chosen in about 6 % of
  positions, no side defends more than 30 % of its turns, and no extra draws appear.
- **In the AI matches** (the computer defends with the weakest character, at random, never twice
  in a row), × 1.25 halved the cost of heavy defending (LEFT wins 32 % instead of 18 %, draws 5 %
  instead of 20 %), while never defending still won most (60 %). So it does not make Defend
  dominant.

**The sentence a player can now say:** *"My Knight can't hit anything useful this turn and the
enemy can reach it, so I defend now: it takes half damage, our cover is guarded, and next turn
its Attack hits 25 % harder (25 instead of 18 against their Knight)."*

## 5. Scenarios checked

| Scenario | With the proposal |
|---|---|
| Full HP vs low HP | At full HP, Attack is still usually right (tempo). At low HP, when the enemy's next hit would defeat the character but half of it would not, Defend saves it and Guarded Strike returns part of the lost tempo. |
| Defend vs Attack now | Defend + Guarded Strike beats Attack + Attack roughly when the chance the enemy hits *this* character × half its damage ≥ your hit chance × (2 × normal − bonus damage); e.g. a Knight against a Knight needs that product ≥ 11 × p HP instead of ≥ 18 × p today. |
| Cover / no cover | Behind cover your hit chance this turn is low, so Attack is worth less and Defend relatively more; in the open both sides hit more and Attack dominates (the 1v1 table at p 0.8 shows little Defend). |
| Normal attacks vs specials | No stacking with Power Shot; Rally unchanged; Repair unchanged. |
| Player vs AI | The AI's search already values a stronger shot (it imagines shots with the real rules). Its Defend decisions (C4 rates) are unchanged and already obey the anti-stall rule. |
| Early vs late rounds | Late in a match the anti-stall rule stops a losing team from defending every turn to reach the round-30 draw. |
| Near round 30 | Today a losing team can stall (up to 7 % extra draws in duels); with the rule it can defend at most every other turn. |
| Repeated Defend | Not possible on consecutive turns; alternating Defend and Guarded Strike is allowed and is the intended rhythm (measured: about 6 % of positions). |

## 7. Tests that would be added

Defend still halves damage (C5) and lasts one enemy action; Guarded Strike is granted only by a
basic Defend (not Rally); it raises the next normal Attack to ATK × 1.25 with exact numbers
(Knight → Knight 25, Archer → Archer 46; halved against a defender); it is used up by that
Attack; it expires if another character acts, if the defender uses its special, or at the end of
that turn; it never stacks; Power Shot neither uses nor adds it; block damage is unchanged;
Defend is refused on the turn after a Defend, with a reason, for both sides and both first
movers; the AI never plans a refused Defend; a rematch and new teams clear it; it interacts
correctly with the last turn of round 30 and with an elimination on that turn; EXP unchanged;
the panel shows it and DEFEND's disabled reason.

## 8. Risks and course compliance

- **Course:** C5 (−50 %) and C4 (the AI defends more below half HP) still hold. Guarded Strike and
  the anti-stall rule are **our additions** and would be listed under custom rules; neither has
  been approved by the instructor.
- **The anti-stall rule removes an option players have today** (defending every turn). It is the
  part most worth the PM's attention; without it, a × 1.25 bonus invites stalling (measured).
- **The duels are a model:** a fixed hit chance, no cover HP, no EXP; the AI matches use the AI's
  simple Defend policy, not a skilled player's. Human play-testing should confirm the feel.
- **Smaller alternative:** × 1.15 with no other change is safe but, as measured, too weak to
  change decisions.
