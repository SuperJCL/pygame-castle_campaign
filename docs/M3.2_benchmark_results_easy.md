# M3.2 balance benchmark results

Generated by `python scripts/balance_benchmark.py --difficulties easy --variants A,B,C150,D150 --decision-variants A,B,C150,D150 --matches 24 --samples 24` in 24 minutes. The method is explained in docs/M3.2_balance_report.md.

Rule variants:

- **A  Baseline (M3.1 rules)**
- **B  Castle Guard**
- **C  Repair 150**
- **D  Castle Guard + Repair 150 (adopted)**

## Strategies against each other

1344 matches, at most 40 rounds each. Win rate = wins + ½ × (draws and unfinished), with a 95 % margin of error. Every match uses mirrored rosters.

## Decision points: the defensive action versus attacking at the same moment

Each sample copies one match at a decision point and plays both choices to the end with the same AIs and random state. Value = win rate after the defensive action − win rate after attacking (positive = the defensive action was the better move). ± is a 95 % margin of error of the paired difference.
