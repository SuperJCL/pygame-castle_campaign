# Castle Siege — course compliance checklist (M3.3)

This checklist compares the game with the CS5001 requirements recorded in this project:
`docs/M0_design.md` §8 (rubric), §9 (requirement map), §4.8 (the instructor's example
formulas) and §10.1 (differences C1–C7). The full rubric text is not in the repository, so this
is our reading of it. **No custom rule listed here has been approved by the instructor**; the
ones that need a decision are marked.

Status:
- **Verified**: built, and checked by automated tests (`python -m unittest discover tests`) or
  scripted runs of the real game.
- **Awaiting manual check**: built, and the automated tests pass, but a person still has to
  play-test it. Human gameplay acceptance is pending for the whole game.
- **Missing**: not done yet (planned for M5).

## Custom rules (differ from the course's examples or add to them)

These are our design rules. None has been approved by the instructor; each can be changed in
one place if the instructor asks.

| Rule | Ours | Course example / requirement | Where to change it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Castle victory (C2) | A castle at 60 % destruction also loses, as well as elimination | Elimination victory | `Battle(castle_loss_enabled=False)` |
| Round limit and tie-break (C7, M3.3) | A team match ends after 30 complete rounds. Tie-breakers: more living characters, then a higher share of character HP, then more castle HP left, else a draw | No round limit mentioned | `battle.ROUND_LIMIT` |
| Draws | Possible only through the round limit | Win or lose | (follows from C7) |
| Damage formula (§4.8) | ATK − DEF, minimum 1; deterministic, so the tests can check exact numbers | ATK − DEF + a random −5 to +10 | `calculate_damage` |
| Defend (C5) and Castle Guard (M3.2) | Defend halves the defender's damage after DEF. While a team defends, enemy shots also do half damage to its castle blocks | Defend reduces damage by 50 % | `CASTLE_GUARD_PERCENT = 0` turns Castle Guard off |
| Attacks can miss (C3) | Characters attack through the team's cannon, aimed with angle and power | May assume every attack hits | — |
| How characters attack (C6) | The acting character "operates" the team's single cannon; its stats set the damage | Characters attack | — |
| AI defence (C4) | Probabilistic: the higher Defend chance applies when any living AI character is below half HP. The weakest one then defends, or the Knight Rallies | "AI defends below 50 % HP" | `DIFFICULTY_SETTINGS` (set the chance to 100 %) |
| Engineer Repair (M3.2) | Up to 150 castle HP per use; cooldown 4; destroyed blocks stay destroyed | Not specified | `Engineer.REPAIR_POOL` |
| Who moves first (M3.3) | LEFT in the first match; the first mover alternates on every rematch | Not specified | `Game.rematch` |
